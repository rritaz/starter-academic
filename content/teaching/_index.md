### Courses

#### University of Illinois Chicago

##### School of Public Health

- **BSTT 535: Categorical Data Analysis**  
  *Spring 2026 | Graduate level*  
  Covers contingency-table analysis, measures of association, stratified analysis, logistic and Poisson regression, generalized linear and log-linear models, matched data, marginal homogeneity, and methods for ordinal outcomes.

- **IPHS 454–455: Quantitative Methods and Analysis I & II**  
  *Fall 2025–Spring 2026 | Graduate level*  
  Two-course sequence providing a foundation in quantitative and research methods for evidence-based public health, including study design, descriptive and inferential statistics, regression, analysis of variance, and interpretation of applied research findings.

- **IPHS 405: Analytic and Research Methods in Public Health, Part II**  
  *Spring 2025 | Graduate level*  
  Second course in a sequence introducing analytical and research methods used in public health, with emphasis on selecting appropriate analyses, evaluating evidence, and interpreting results.

- **IPHS 402: Analytic and Research Methods in Public Health**  
  *Fall 2023 and Fall 2024 | Graduate level*  
  Introduces study design, data description, statistical inference, and research methods used to carry out the core functions of evidence-based public health.

##### College of Applied Health Sciences

- **AHS 511–512: Biostatistics I & II**  
  *Fall 2023–2025; Spring 2024–2026 | Graduate level*  
  Two-course sequence covering biostatistical reasoning and applied data analysis. Topics include descriptive statistics, probability, one- and two-sample inference, ANOVA and ANCOVA, linear and logistic regression, clinical-trial design, nonparametric and categorical methods, factor and cluster analysis, and longitudinal and repeated-measures approaches.
